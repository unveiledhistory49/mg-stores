For MARKSONGLOBAL (MG) STORES, I recommend positioning it as a modern nationwide supermarket that sells both provisions and electronics, while keeping the electronics as a major category rather than making the whole site look like an electronics shop.

🎨 Brand direction

Brand name: MARKSONGLOBAL (MG) STORES

Tagline: “Everything You Need. One Store.”

Market: Nationwide — Nigeria

Style: Modern • Premium • Trustworthy • Affordable • Nigerian

For the colour, I would choose a deep emerald green + warm gold + white combination. It gives you a supermarket feel while still looking premium enough for electronics.

⭐ The most important design idea

Don't make the website look like “a provisions website that also happens to sell electronics.”

Make it look like a large Nigerian digital supermarket where customers immediately understand:

Food + Provisions + Household + Electronics + Everyday Essentials = MG STORES

And I'd make the MG logo very prominent so that eventually people recognize MG even without seeing the full MARKSONGLOBAL name.
